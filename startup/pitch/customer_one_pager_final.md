### Use Case: Healthcare

**Shared iPad for Nurse Check-Ins**

*Challenge:* 12 iPads in a hospital wing—anyone can access, HIPAA risk.

*Solution:*
1. Nurse taps badge → device unlocks with nurse-specific apps
2. Device checks MDM posture (encryption, OS current)
3. If compromised → auto-quarantine + ITSM ticket
4. Audit log proves who accessed what, when
5. Nurse walks away → device auto-locks in <5 seconds

**ROI:**
- Saves 72 min/day (2 min × 12 devices × 3 shifts)
- Achieves HIPAA compliance
- Reduces IT tickets by 40%

### Use Case: Retail
