# SignalGrid 12-Month Startup Roadmap

## Overview

This roadmap outlines SignalGrid's journey from product completion to Series A readiness over 12 months.

**Goal:** Establish product-market fit, acquire first enterprise customers, and raise seed funding.

---

## Phase 1: Product Completion (Months 1-3)

### Month 1: Foundation

**Objective:** Complete core platform features and prepare for internal demos.

**Milestones:**
- [ ] Device Identity Graph — Core correlation engine
- [ ] Risk Scoring Engine — Basic scoring model
- [ ] Policy Engine — Rule-based evaluation
- [ ] Initial Integrations — ServiceNow, Splunk

**Deliverables:**
- Working internal demo environment
- API documentation draft
- Product one-pager

**Team:**
- CEO (full-time)
- CTO (full-time)
- 2 Engineers (hire or contract)

---

### Month 2: Security & Automation

**Objective:** Add security-focused features and automation capabilities.

**Milestones:**

## Risks & Mitigation

### Risk: Slow Customer Adoption
**Mitigation:** Increase outbound sales, attend industry events, develop partner channel

### Risk: Competitive Pressure
**Mitigation:** Focus on differentiation (automation-first, vendor-neutral), build switching costs

### Risk: Technical Challenges
**Mitigation:** Prioritize stability over features, invest in QA, maintain technical debt runway

### Risk: Funding Environment
**Mitigation:** Maintain lean operations, demonstrate metrics, expand investor network
