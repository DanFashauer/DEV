## Problem Section

### The Device Security Challenge

Modern enterprises manage thousands of mobile devices, IoT sensors, and connected endpoints. But here's the problem:

**Your security tools don't talk to each other.**

Device identity lives in your MDM. Authentication logs live in your IAM. Security events live in your SIEM. Location data lives somewhere else entirely.

**The result?**
- Blind spots where attacks hide
- Manual, slow incident response
- Incomplete device visibility
- Compliance gaps

**67% of security breaches involve unmanaged or poorly managed devices.**

## How It Works

### Three Steps to Automated Security

#### 1. Connect Your Signals

Deploy badge readers, configure MDM integration, enable location services. SignalGrid collects signals from every device touchpoint.

```
Badge Scan → Device Identity → Policy Evaluation → Action
Location Update → Risk Score → Automated Response → Notification
```

#### 2. Evaluate in Real-Time

Our policy engine evaluates every signal against configurable rules. Risk scoring runs continuously.

- Device posture compliance
- Location authorization
- Authentication patterns
- Behavioral anomalies

#### 3. Automate Response

When signals cross thresholds, automated actions fire instantly:

- **Quarantine** — Isolate compromised devices
- **Alert** — Notify security teams via Slack, email, PagerDuty
- **Ticket** — Create ITSM incidents automatically
- **Log** — Forward to SIEM for forensics
- **Lock** — Revoke session access

## Use Cases

### Industry Solutions

---

#### Healthcare

**Secure Shared Clinical Devices**

Hospitals and clinics have thousands of shared mobile devices used by clinicians across shifts. SignalGrid ensures:

- Only authorized clinicians can access devices
- Devices comply with security policies before use
- Compromised devices are quarantined instantly
- Audit trails meet HIPAA requirements

**Example:** A nurse's badge scan instantly validates device assignment, checks compliance posture, and launches the correct clinical application. If the device fails security checks, access is denied and IT is alerted.

---

#### Retail

**Protect Store Devices and Customer Data**

Retail environments have POS systems, inventory scanners, and customer-facing tablets. SignalGrid provides:

- Device identity tied to employee badges
- Location-based access controls
- Real-time compliance monitoring
- Automated incident response

**Example:** A lost tablet is detected outside authorized locations. SignalGrid immediately quarantines the device, creates a helpdesk ticket, and logs the event to the SIEM.

---

#### Logistics

**Monitor Warehouse Device Fleets**

Warehouses and distribution centers rely on hundreds of scanners, tablets, and sensors. SignalGrid delivers:

- Complete device inventory and identity
- Location tracking within facilities
- Posture compliance enforcement
- Automated device provisioning

**Example:** A scanner with outdated firmware attempts to connect. SignalGrid detects the posture failure, blocks network access, and creates a ticket for the IT team.
