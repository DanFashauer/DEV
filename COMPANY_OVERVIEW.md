# Company Overview: SignalGrid

## Executive summary

**SignalGrid is a Zero Trust orchestration platform for shared and mobile work environments.**

**Mission:** SignalGrid enables secure, contextual, real-time access by continuously evaluating identity, device trust, and operational context.

**Category framing:** SignalGrid sits between identity, device management, and operational workflows to make trust decisions at the moment of use.

## Company

**SignalGrid** builds Zero Trust orchestration for shared and mobile work environments.

## Mission

SignalGrid enables secure, contextual, real-time access by continuously evaluating identity, device trust, and operational context.

## Vision

Enable every shared or mobile endpoint to make trust-aware access decisions that are fast for users, reliable for operations, and accountable for security teams.

## Why now

Frontline organizations operate with rising cyber risk, more shared-device workflows, and stricter accountability requirements. Existing stacks often separate identity, device posture, and enforcement, creating decision gaps at the point of use.

## Category we are creating / occupying

SignalGrid operates in the **Zero Trust orchestration** category for shared and mobile workflows. It connects trust inputs, policy decisions, and downstream enforcement into a single operational loop.

## Values

- **Trust is earned, never assumed**
- **Security without workflow friction**
- **Operational clarity over tool sprawl**
- **Real-world context matters**
- **Build for resilience and accountability**

## Executive value framing

SignalGrid is designed to support board- and CIO-level outcomes:
- Reduce risk through posture-aware access controls and explicit trust decisions.
- Improve operations by making shared-device workflows predictable and enforceable.
- Increase productivity through lower access friction in frontline environments.
- Strengthen compliance and resilience through auditable governance and enforcement evidence.
- Differentiate organizations with context-aware trust orchestration across identity, device, and operations.

## Ideal customer profile

Organizations with regulated or high-consequence frontline operations that rely on shared or mobile devices, including healthcare systems, logistics operators, and similar environments where real-time access decisions must be secure and auditable.

## Near-term focus

- Pilot-readiness for real-world frontline environments.
- Authentication and policy hardening.
- Stronger trust consistency across session and admin surfaces.
- Integration reliability and governance visibility.

## Long-term platform vision

A resilient trust orchestration platform that continuously fuses identity, device posture, location, and operational context into explainable decisions and deterministic actions across the broader security and operations stack.
