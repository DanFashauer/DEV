# SignalGrid Claim Boundaries

Purpose: keep external messaging strong and truthful while separating shipped behavior from roadmap and concept work.

Category thesis remains unchanged:
- **The Decision Layer for Enterprise Risk**
- **Others detect. SignalGrid decides.**

## What marketing can say now

- SignalGrid makes real-time access decisions using identity, posture, and context.
- SignalGrid enforces allow/deny/action outcomes and records audit-ready decision trails.
- SignalGrid integrates with enterprise systems (identity, endpoint, SIEM, ITSM, NAC pathways) to operationalize decisions.
- SignalGrid is designed for shared-device and frontline workflows where delayed decisions create risk and friction.

## What investor deck can say now

- The category wedge is a decision layer between signal and impact.
- Initial beachhead: high-risk shared-device and endpoint trust workflows.
- The product converts fragmented trust signals into immediate policy outcomes.
- Integration-led adoption supports land-and-expand without rip-and-replace.

## Must be framed as roadmap

- Deeper client authenticity weighting across all policy contexts.
- Broader attestation/certificate evidence normalization and confidence scoring.
- Expanded physical access ecosystem interoperability (provider breadth + mobile credentials).
- Decision-memory and remediation playbook automation.

## Architecture/future-notes only (not current product claims)

- Autonomous policy suggestions and self-tuning trust policies.
- Fully unified physical+digital trust fabric across all enterprise surfaces.
- Hardware-backed trust as a universal, always-available gating primitive in every workflow.
