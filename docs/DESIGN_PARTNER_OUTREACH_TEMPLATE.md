## Ideal target profiles (early design partners)
- **Endpoint engineering:** teams responsible for device posture, enrollment standards, and endpoint risk operations.
- **Enterprise mobility:** owners of shared/mobile fleet workflows where access friction directly impacts operations.
- **Identity/security architecture:** leaders defining access policy, session controls, and Zero Trust design patterns.
- **Zero Trust program owners:** cross-functional teams accountable for measurable trust outcomes across identity + device signals.
- **Regulated/shared-device environments:** healthcare, logistics, field operations, and other settings with high auditability and workflow uptime requirements.

## Messaging reminders (keep outreach MVP-scoped)
- **Additive to Intune/Entra:** position SignalGrid as a decision layer above existing Microsoft controls, not a replacement.
- **Closed-loop remediation before workflow disruption:** emphasize detect → remediation attempt → re-evaluate → final access outcome before workflows break.
- **MVP scope only:** avoid broad enterprise claims; stay anchored to current session-start closed-loop capabilities.

## Quick usage notes
- Prioritize 15 targets and keep each entry current after every touchpoint.
- Set one clear next step per contact (follow-up date, intro ask, or meeting target).
- Review status twice weekly to keep momentum and avoid stalled threads.
